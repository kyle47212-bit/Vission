👁 What “insider tracking” means here

Public wallet watch
Instead of claiming access to private insider information, this radar can flag publicly visible on-chain behavior: large-wallet transfers, unusual accumulation, holder concentration, new wallets entering, and clusters of wallets moving together.
Important
Wallet activity does not prove someone has non-public information. A whale can be an investor, market maker, team wallet, exchange, or unrelated holder. The radar should surface activity for research—not promise a 100× outcome.
